In ten years we have gone from crude systems which produce relatively clean looking but gibberish language, to systems capable of writing (bad) poetry, and rudimentary computer programs (with GPT-3), to systems which can solve PhD level mathematics problems, write sophisticated computer programs, better than nearly all humans would ever be able to, work on very long horizon tasks.

Some people still deny these systems are powerful. However, after apprasing their capabilities seriously, and spending significant time using them on a day to day basis, for me to conclude that systems that exist today outperform most humans on a wide variety of tasks. I began to believe this in the aftermath of the release of GPT "reasoning" models in mid-2025. Progress since mid-2025 has accelerated, not slowed, and every talented software engineer I know has functionally automated the vast majority of the work that previously comprised their time.

In my (fairly uninformed view), there is no reason to expect that progress will abate in the near future, and barring some unforseen bottleneck, AI system using the standard transformer architecture will continue to grow more advanced.
